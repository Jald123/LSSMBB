Fishbone Diagram AI BRAINSTORM

Root Cause Analysis. The 6 Ms. Find the source of the problem.

🔍 What Is It?
A visual tool (Cause-and-Effect Diagram) to brainstorm potential causes of a problem.
⏱️ When To Use?
During the Analyze phase, when you know WHAT is wrong but not WHY.
💡 Why Use It?
Prevents "Tunnel Vision" by forcing you to look at 6 different categories of causes.
📋 The 6 Ms
Man, Machine, Method, Material, Measurement, Mother Nature (Environment).
📐 Structure
The "Head" is the problem (Effect). The "Bones" are the categories of causes.
🧠 Brainstorming
Use with a team. Ask "Why?" for each branch to drill down deeper.
🎯 Next Steps
After listing causes, circle the top 3 "Likely Suspects" to investigate with data.
🚀 Phase Relevance
Analyze: Generate hypotheses to test with statistics (e.g. t-Test).
