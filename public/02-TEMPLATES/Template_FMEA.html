FMEA Tool AI RISK MANAGER

Failure Modes & Effects Analysis. Predict the Future. Fix it before it breaks.

🔍 What Is It?
A systematic tool to identify where a process might fail and how bad it would be.
⏱️ When To Use?
Before implementing a new process, or when analyzing risks in a current one.
💡 Why Use It?
Prioritizes risks mathematically (RPN) so you focus resources on the biggest threats first.
📋 Formula
RPN = Severity × Occurrence × Detection (Scale 1-10).
📐 Severity (SEV)
1 = No Effect.
10 = Hazardous/Safety Issue (Catastrophic).
🧠 Occurrence (OCC)
1 = Extremely unlikely (1 in a million).
10 = Inevitable (Every day).
🎯 Detection (DET)
1 = Certain to catch it (Auto-Stop).
10 = Cannot be detected until customer gets it.
🚀 Phase Relevance
Analyze: Identify risks.
Improve: Implement controls to lower RPN.
