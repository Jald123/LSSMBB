📜 Origin & Power

One of the 7 Basic Tools of Quality. Takes raw columns of numbers and reveals the "personality" of your process—center, spread, and shape.

🤔 What & Why?

A bar chart of continuous data frequency. Used to detect Non-Normality, bi-modal mixtures, and outliers before analysis.

📏 The Standard

Bins = Sqrt(n). Bars must touch. Bell Shape = Normal. Double Peak = Bi-modal. Skewed = Limiting boundary.

Strict Roles

  • Green Belt: Creates & Interprets.
  • Black Belt: Analyzing advanced non-normal shapes.
