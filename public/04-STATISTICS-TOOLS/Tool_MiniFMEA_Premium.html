Risk Scan (Mini-FMEA) MEASURE

Rapid Failure Analysis. Proactive Risk Shield. What could go wrong?

🔍 What Is It?
A rapid risk assessment tool focused on the most critical failure points in a process.
⏱️ When To Use?
Early in a project (MEASURE/ANALYZE) to identify where things could go wrong.
💡 Why Use It?
To proactively catch system failures before they happen without a full FMEA overhead.
📋 Requirements
Process steps, potential failures, severity/likelihood guesses, and mitigation.
📐 RPN Formula
Sev x Lik x Det (Severity x Likelihood x Detection). Scale 1-10.
🧠 Interpretation
Critical Risks (>100 RPN) require immediate action plans or design changes.
🎯 Action Threshold
Industry Std: High (>100), Medium (40-99), Low (<40).< /div>
🚀 Phase Relevance
Measure: ID Risks.
Analyze: Prioritize Issues.
