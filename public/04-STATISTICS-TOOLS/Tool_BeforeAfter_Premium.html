Verification Tool IMPROVE

Before/After Comparison. ROI Validation. Prove the shift.

🔍 What Is It?
A performance comparison tool used to verify the success of a pilot or full implementation.
⏱️ When To Use?
During IMPROVE / CONTROL phase to prove the 'After' is better than the 'Before'.
💡 Why Use It?
To demonstrate ROI, process improvement, and stability to stakeholders.
📋 Requirements
Baseline (Before) and Post-Improvement (After) data for the same specific metric.
📐 Delta Formula
|Before - After| / Before. Shows the percentage reduction/gain.
🧠 Interpretation
A significant shift proves project success. Ensure stability is also maintained.
🎯 Target Result
Typical goals: >50% reduction in waste or >25% increase in throughput.
🚀 Phase Relevance
Improve: Validate Pilot.
Control: Sustain Gains.
