The Sigma Equation

DPMO =
Total Defects Units × Opps
× 1,000,000
Sigma (Z) = NormInv(1 - DPMO1,000,000) + 1.5

The +1.5 Shift accounts for long-term process variation.

Abbreviation Breakdown

• DPMO: Defects Per Million Opportunities
• Opps: Opportunities for error per process unit
• Z: Sigma Level (Z-score)
• NormInv: Converts probability into Z-score (Excel: =NORM.S.INV()). e.g., 93.3% Yield = 1.5 Z
• 1M: Normalizing factor (1,000,000)
• 1.5 Shift: Long-term variation adjustment
