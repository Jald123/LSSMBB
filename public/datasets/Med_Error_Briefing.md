# MISSION BRIEFING: Medication Error Reduction
**Sector:** Clinical Safety / Quality Assurance
**Lead Sensei:** Jald123 / LSSMBB
**Classification:** HIGH ALERT PROTOCOL

## 1. Executive Summary
St. Jude’s Medical Center has reported a statistically significant increase in "Near Miss" events and actual dispensing errors involving high-alert medications (Heparin, Insulin). Current DPMO (Defects Per Million Opportunities) is approximately 6,200 (approx. 4-Sigma). Our target is 6-Sigma (3.4 DPMO).

## 2. Project Charter (Tactical Baseline)
*   **Problem Statement:** Medication dispensing errors in the ICU have increased by 22% year-over-year, leading to 4 serious adverse drug events in the last 12 months.
*   **Goal:** Achieved 0 actual errors and <1% near-miss rate for high-alert medications through Fail-Safe (Poka-Yoke) implementation.
*   **KPIs:** Errors per 1,000 doses, Near-miss reporting rate, Nurse audit scores.

## 3. High-Alert Focus
The primary failure points appear to be during the **Dispensing** and **Administration** phases, specifically during manual calculation of pediatric doses.

## 4. Operation Intelligence
Refer to `Med_Error_Data.csv`. Use **Pareto Analysis** to identify the top categories of errors.

## 5. Mission Objectives
1.  **Define:** Create a Swimlane process map of the medication journey from pharmacy to patient.
2.  **Measure:** Perform a Gage R&R on the error reporting system.
3.  **Analyze:** Use a Fishbone diagram to explore Root Causes (e.g., Interruptions, Fatigue, Labeling).
4.  **Improve:** Implement bar-code medication administration (BCMA) and standard work.
5.  **Control:** Establish a Mistake-Proofing protocol with double-verification steps.
