📊 SIPOC Diagram (Suppliers, Inputs, Process, Outputs, Customers)

Define Phase • Yellow Belt

SUPPLIERS → INPUTS → PROCESS → OUTPUTS → CUSTOMERS
Suppliers → Inputs → Process (✓ Focus Area) → Outputs → Customers

Guiding Questions

1. Who are the SUPPLIERS?

List all internal and external sources that provide inputs to your process.

Examples: Vendors, other departments, customers, systems
2. What are the key INPUTS?

List the materials, information, or services that enter your process.

Examples: Raw materials, data, forms, requests, customer information
3. What is the PROCESS?

Describe the main steps or activities in your process (in 2-5 words).

Examples: Invoice Processing, Order Fulfillment, Customer Service
4. What are the key OUTPUTS?

What does your process produce or deliver?

Examples: Processed invoices, delivered products, resolved issues
5. Who are the CUSTOMERS?

Who uses or receives your outputs (internal or external customers)?

Examples: Finance team, external clients, warehouse, next process step
