1. What is Lean Six Sigma?

Lean Six Sigma is a business management strategy that combines the principles of Lean (eliminating waste) and Six Sigma (reducing variation) to improve processes and reduce defects.

2. History & Evolution

1980s - Six Sigma Origins: Motorola introduced Six Sigma to reduce defects in manufacturing. The goal was to achieve 99.99966% quality.

1990s - Toyota Lean: Toyota developed Lean principles to eliminate waste in production.

2000s - Integration: Companies combined Lean and Six Sigma for maximum benefit.

3. Key Terminology

Term Definition
Defect Any deviation from customer requirements
DMAIC Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control
Variation Inconsistency in process output
Sigma Level Measure of process capability (higher = fewer defects)
