SIPOC STRATEGY

Master the High-Level Process Architecture.

The Role of SIPOC

The SIPOC is the first tool used in the Define phase of DMAIC. It prevents "scope creep" by clearly defining the boundaries (Start and Stop points) of the process. If it's not on the SIPOC, it's not in the project.

ORDER MATTERS

Most pros fill it out as P-O-C-I-S. Start with the Process, then look at what it produces (Outputs) and who gets them (Customers).

30,000 FEET

A SIPOC should have no more than 7 steps. If you have 20 steps, you are doing a "Process Map", not a SIPOC.

🥧 THE BAKING ANALOGY

Suppliers: Grocery Store. Inputs: Egg, Flour. Process: Mix, Bake. Outputs: Cake. Customers: Party Guests.
