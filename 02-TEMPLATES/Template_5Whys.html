PHASE: ANALYZE

5 Whys Analysis AI DRILL DOWN

Dig Deeper. Find the Root. Don't stop at the symptom.

🔍 What Is It?
An iterative interrogation technique to explore cause-and-effect relationships.
⏱️ When To Use?
For simple to moderate problems. (For complex ones, use Fishbone or DOE).
💡 Why Use It?
Most "solutions" only fix symptoms (Band-Aids). 5 Whys gets to the root infection.
📋 The Method
State the problem, then ask "Why did that happen?" 5 times (or until you find the root).
1️⃣ Why?
"The fuse blew." (Symptom)
3️⃣ Why?
"The bearing was dry." (Mechanism)
5️⃣ Why?
"No lubrication schedule existed." (Root Cause - Systemic).
🚀 Phase Relevance
Analyze: Verify the root cause before fixing it.
