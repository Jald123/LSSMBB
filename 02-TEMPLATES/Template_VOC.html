PHASE: DEFINE

Voice of Customer (VOC) AI ANALYST

Translate Feedback. Define Quality. Turn complaints into specs.

🔍 What Is It?
A tool to capture customer requirements and translate them into measurable goals.
⏱️ When To Use?
In the Define phase, to ensure the project solves the CUSTOMER'S problem, not just yours.
💡 Why Use It?
Customers speak in vague terms ("It's too slow"). You need specific targets ("< 5 mins").
📋 The Flow
Verbatim (Quote) → Key Driver (Need) → CTQ (Metric).
🗣️ Verbatim
"The coffee is always cold when I get it!" (The raw complaint).
🔑 Key Driver
"Temperature Requirement" (The underlying need).
📏 CTQ
"Coffee Temp > 165°F upon delivery" (Critical to Quality spec).

Define: Creates the "Y" for your Y=f(x) equation.
