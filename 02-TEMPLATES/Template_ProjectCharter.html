PHASE: DEFINE

Project Charter AI WRITER

Define the Mission. Align the Team. The single most important document.

🔍 What Is It?
A formal document that authorizes a project, stating the problem, goal, scope, and team.
⏱️ When To Use?
At the very start (Day 1). It must be approved by a Champion before work begins.
💡 Why Use It?
Prevents "Scope Creep" and ensures everyone agrees on what "Success" looks like.
📋 Key Elements
Problem Statement, Business Case, Goal Statement, Scope (In/Out), Timeline.
📐 The 5 W's
Who, What, Where, When, Why (but NEVER 'How' - that's for Improve phase).
🧠 SMART Goals
Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
🎯 Reference Values
A good Problem Statement quantifies the pain: "Costing $50k/month" vs "Too expensive".
🚀 Phase Relevance
Define: The outputs of this step guide Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control.
