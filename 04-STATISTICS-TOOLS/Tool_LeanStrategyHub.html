👧 Hoshin Kanri Simple Explanation

🤔 WHAT is it?

Hoshin Kanri is a Japanese strategic planning method. Think of it as a GPS for your organization – it ensures everyone drives towards the same destination.

💡 WHY use it?

To eliminate "Strategic Drift". Without it, leadership sets goals but teams work on unrelated tasks. The X-Matrix forces clear connections.

1. SOUTH (Strategy)
3-5 Year "Breakthrough" vision. Focus on 'Where do we want to be?'
Examples:
  • Be #1 Regional Provider
  • Double Annual Revenue
  • Achieve Zero Harm
⮕
2. WEST (Objectives)
Annual Goals. Breaking the vision into measurable 1-year targets.
Examples:
  • Increase Sales by 15%
  • Reduce Wait by 25%
  • Certify 50 Green Belts
⮕
3. NORTH (Projects)
Tactical Priorities. These are the specific projects to hit annual goals.
Examples:
  • Launch CRM System
  • Redesign Intake Flow
  • Supplier Audit Program
⮕
4. EAST (Metrics)
Results / KPIs. How we track the success of our projects.
Examples:
  • Net Promoter Score
  • Cycle Time (min)
  • Defect Rate (PPM)
🥋 MBB Alignment Tip: If you have a project in North that doesn't clearly support an objective in West, it's considered "Strategic Waste".
