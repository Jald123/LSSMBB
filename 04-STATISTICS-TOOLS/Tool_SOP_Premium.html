SOP STRATEGIC INSIGHT

HISTORY OF ORIGIN

Rooted in Frederick Taylor's Scientific Management (1900s). Refined by Lean into "Visual Standards" where instructions must be simple and visual.

WHAT IS IT?

An SOP is a set of step-by-step instructions compiled by an organization to help workers carry out complex routine operations.

WHY USE IT?

To achieve efficiency, quality output, and uniformity of performance, while reducing miscommunication and failure to comply with standards.

WHEN TO ADD?

Created during the Control phase to ensure the improved process remains stable and sustainable over time.
