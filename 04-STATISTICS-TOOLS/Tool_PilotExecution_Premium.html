DAILY LIFE ANALOGY

The "Morning Routine" Pilot ☕

You designed a new routine to save 20 mins every morning. Before you force your whole family to do it, you must "DO" a pilot.

🛠️ The Training Matrix

You have to 'train' your partner not to talk to you until after the second step. If they don't mastery this, your pilot data is useless!

⏱️ The Pilot Run Log

You track your time for 3 days. Day 1: 15 mins saved. Day 2: 18 mins. Day 3: 10 mins (The alarm didn't go off).

⚠️ The Friction Log

Observation: The coffee machine takes 4 mins to warm up, creating a bottleneck. Resistance: The kids missed 'cartoon time' and are grumpy.

🎯 The Decision (Check)

The pilot proved the routine works, but the coffee machine "Friction" must be solved before we scale this to the whole house.

STUDENT TAKEAWAY: A pilot isn't just about data; it's about finding out why the plan might fail in the real world.
