RACI Matrix Explained
You and your team are launching a New Medical App. Without RACI, everyone is doing everything, but nothing is getting signed off!
🤯
Problem 1: Too Many Cooks
Multiple Accountables

If 3 people are 'Accountable' for data privacy, blame is shifted when a breach occurs. Rule: Exactly 1 'A' per row.

🦗
Problem 2: The Bystander
The Missing Responsibility

A task with only 'Informed' roles will never finish. Someone must be the 'Doer'. Rule: At least 1 'R' per row.

Origin

The RACI model evolved from the Linear Responsibility Chart (LRC) developed in the 1950s. It was designed to bridge the gap between organizational charts (who reports to whom) and process flows (who does what).

Evolution

In the 1970s, it became a cornerstone of Project Management as projects became more cross-functional. It was later adopted by Lean Six Sigma as a primary Governance tool.

🏥 Medication Administration Workflow

In a clinical setting, clarity saves lives. RACI ensures that while a Tech might be Responsible for delivery, the Nurse is Accountable for verification, and the Pharmacist is Consulted on drug interaction.
