The Origin Story
"In 1914, Donaldson Brown (DuPont) invented the ROI formula. He realized that profit wasn't just about sales—it was about 'Turnover'. This formula allowed GM to manage decentralized divisions and became the bedrock of modern corporate finance."

The 3 Buckets of Savings

Hard Savings
Direct impact on the P&L (Profit & Loss). Real cash removed from the budget.
$ Cash

Ex: Reducing 5 FTEs (through attrition), scraping less material.

Soft Savings
Efficiency gains that don't immediately reduce budget but increase capacity.
Efficiency

Ex: Reducing RN documentation time by 20% (they still get paid same salary).

Cost Avoidance
Spending that would have happened if you didn't act.
Prevention

Ex: Avoiding a CMS penalty for readmissions. Delaying a new MRI purchase.

Case Study: The Surgical Robot

Scenario: Surgery Dept wants a $1.2M robot. They claim it saves 20 mins per case.
The Trap: Saving 20 minutes is a SOFT saving if you don't reduce staff or do MORE cases. Is the OR utilized 100%? No.
The Fix: MBB realized the time savings allowed 1 extra case per day (Revenue Generation).
New Calculation: 1 case/day * $5k margin * 250 days = $1.25M/year Hard Revenue.
Result: ROI = 104% in Year 1. Approved.

Financial Impact Philosophy

What is it?

ROI (Return on Investment) measures the gain or loss generated on an investment relative to the amount of money invested. It is expressed as a percentage.

Why use it?

To prioritize projects based on their financial viability and to secure executive sponsorship by proving the value of Lean Six Sigma initiatives.

General Example: Manufacturing

A plant invests $200k in a new automated sorting machine. The machine saves $100k/year in labor and reduces scrap by $50k/year. Over 3 years, the total benefit is $450k. ROI = ($450k - $200k) / $200k = 125%.

Healthcare Example: OR Turnaround

A hospital spends $50k on a Lean workshop to reduce OR turnaround time. The time saved allows for 1 extra surgery per day, generating $5k in additional margin. Over 250 days, this is $1.25M in new revenue. ROI = ($1.25M - $50k) / $50k = 2400%.
