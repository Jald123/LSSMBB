One-Way ANOVA AI COACH ENABLED

Analyze Variance. Compare 3+ Groups. Find the hidden factor.

🔍 What Is It?
Tests if the means of 3 or more groups are significantly different by comparing variances.
⏱️ When To Use?
When comparing multiple machines, shifts, or suppliers (e.g. Shift A vs B vs C).
💡 Why Use It?
Prevents "False Positives" that happen if you just run multiple t-Tests back-to-back.
📋 Requirements
Normal Data. Independent Samples. Homogeneity of Variance.
📐 Standard Equation
F = MS_between / MS_within
🧠 Interpretation
P < 0.05: At least ONE group is different.
P > 0.05: All group means are statistically equal.
🎯 Reference Values
High F-Value (>3.0) usually suggests a difference exists.
🚀 Phase Relevance
Analyze: Identify critical X factors.
Improve: Verify multi-site rollout.
