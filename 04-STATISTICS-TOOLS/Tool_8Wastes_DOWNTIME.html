The 8 Predators of Profit

Waste is invisible until you look for it. Every minute of waiting or extra-processing is a direct theft from patient care and your bottom line.

🔗 Alternative Mnemonics

TIMWOOD: The original Toyota 7 wastes (T, I, M, W, O, O, D).
TIMWOODS: Adds 'Skills' to address the modern knowledge workforce.
COMMWIP: A manufacturing variation (Correction, Overproduction, Motion...).

🧠 Key Definitions

Idle Time: Time during which a machine or person is ready for work but not producing due to downstream delays.
Muda: The Japanese term for 'waste' - any activity that consumes resources but adds no value.
Value-Add: Any transformation of material or information that the customer is explicitly willing to pay for.
The Origin Story
"The concept of 'Muda' (Waste) was codified by Taiichi Ohno at Toyota in the 1950s. He famously drew chalk circles on the floor and made managers stand in them for hours to observe waste. The 8th waste (Non-Utilized Talent) was added later to address the Knowledge Economy."
