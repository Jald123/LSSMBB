The Origin Story
"Psychologist Bruce Tuckman published his theory in 1965. He noticed that every single team—whether a jazz band, a surgical unit, or a boardroom—follows the exact same psychological path. He proved that conflict ('Storming') is not a sign of failure, but a necessary step to maturity."
