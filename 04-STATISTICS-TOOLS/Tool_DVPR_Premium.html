🔍 What Is It?
Design Verification Plan & Report. A formal record that tracks testing of a new process or product.
⏱️ When To Use?
During IMPROVE / CONTROL phase to document that all requirements are fully validated.
💡 Why Use It?
To provide objective evidence of performance and 'Pass/Fail' status for official stakeholder sign-off.
📋 Requirements
Test names, target specifications, actual results, and acceptance criteria.
📐 Verification Goal
Prove that the design meets the CTQs (Critical to Quality) identified in the Define phase.
🧠 Interpretation
All tests must be 'PASS' or have an approved deviation before moving to full launch.
🎯 Audit Trail
Provides a defensive record for quality audits and regulatory inspections (ISO, FDA, JCI).
🚀 Phase Relevance
Improve: Pilot Data.
Control: Handover Evidence.
