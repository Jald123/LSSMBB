🎓 The Final Gate MBB Standard

"Certification is not a reward for attendance; it is confirmation of competence." In Lean Six Sigma, the belt isn't just a status symbol—it's a warning to problems that you know how to solve them.

Origin: Inspired by the rigid testing of Motorola (1986) and AlliedSignal. Unlike other fields, LSS Certification usually requires a *completed project* (Hard Savings), not just a test.

The Power of the Transcript:
  • Career Passport: This transcript lists your specific "micro-competencies" (e.g., Gage R&R, DOE). This is what hiring managers look for.
  • Proof of Depth: Proves you didn't just "read the book," but actually deployed the tools.

Certification Protocol

🏆 DON'T BE A "PAPER BELT"

There is a stigma in the industry against "Paper Belts"—people who pass a quiz but can't run a project. Use this tool to generate your transcript ONLY after you have completed your first successful improvement project.

📝 MICRO-CREDENTIALS REVOLUTION

Modern HR departments are moving away from generic degrees. They want to see Stackable Credentials. Your transcript highlighting "Hypothesis Testing" or "Value Stream Mapping" is often more valuable than the generic title "Green Belt."
