Measure Capability. Benchmark Process. How good is your process really?

🔍 What Is It?
Converts your defect rate (DPMO) into a universal "Sigma Score" (0 to 6).
⏱️ When To Use?
At the start (Define) to set a Baseline, and at the end (Control) to prove success.
💡 Why Use It?
Allows you to compare apples-to-oranges (e.g. HR Process vs Factory Line) on one scale.
📋 Requirements
Count of defects, total units processed, and opportunities for error per unit available.
📐 Standard Equation
DPMO = (Defects / Opps) * 1M
Z = NormInv(1 - DPMO/1M) + 1.5
🧠 Interpretation
6σ = World Class (3.4 defects/M).
3σ = Average (66,807 defects/M).
🎯 Reference Values
A 1.5 Sigma Shift is traditionally added to estimating long-term capability.
🚀 Phase Relevance
Define: Problem Statement baseline.
Control: Final validation.

🍕 The Pizza Delivery Scenario

You order a pizza. The promise is 30 minutes or less. Every minute late is a "Defect".

🚚💨

Process A (3 Sigma)

"The Average Shop"

Delivers 66,800 late pizzas for every million orders.

~7% Failure Rate
🚀✨

Process B (6 Sigma)

"World Class"

Delivers only 3.4 late pizzas for every million orders.

99.9997% Success Rate

The Cost of Poor Quality

Sigma Level Yield (Success) DPMO (Defects) Analogy
6 σ 99.9997% 3.4 One misspelled word in all the books in a small library.
5 σ 99.98% 233 One minute of unsafe drinking water every seven months.
4 σ 99.4% 6,210 One hour of unsafe drinking water every month.
3 σ 93.3% 66,807 1.5 hours of unsafe drinking water EVERY DAY.
2 σ 69.1% 308,537 Major crisis. System is broken.
