Box & whisker Plot

ANALYZE
"The quickest way to spot an outlier."

📜 Origin & Power ℹ️

Introduced by John Tukey. Excels at side-by-side comparison and outlier detection.

🧬 Anatomy of a Box Plot

  • Box: Interquartile Range (IQR). Contains middle 50% of data.
  • Whiskers: Range of expected variation (usually 1.5x IQR).
  • Dots: Outliers indicating abnormal special causes.

🤔 What is it? ℹ️

5-Number Summary and Outlier Detector.

👓 Interpretation ℹ️

Box = Middle 50%. Dots = Outliers.

📏 The Standard ℹ️

1.5x IQR Rule for Outliers.

📊 Reference Values ℹ️

Normality checks and expected percentiles.

💡 Pro Tip

For non-normal data, Box Plot is safer than StDev.

📋 Pre-Requirements

Continuous Data. Ideally 20+ points per group for a stable box.

👮 Strict Roles

Green Belt: Compares shifts/machines.
Black Belt: Analyzes residuals in DOE.
