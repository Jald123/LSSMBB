🧬 Anatomy of a Box Plot
● Box: Interquartile Range (IQR). Contains middle 50% of data. ● Whiskers: Range of expected variation (1.5x IQR). ● Dots: Outliers indicating abnormal special causes.
🤔 What is it?
5-Number Summary and Outlier Detector. Excels at side-by-side comparison.
👓 Interpretation
Box = Middle 50%. Dots = Outliers. Median line shows the "Balance" of your data.
📏 The Standard
1.5x IQR Rule for Outliers. For non-normal data, Box Plot is safer than StDev.
