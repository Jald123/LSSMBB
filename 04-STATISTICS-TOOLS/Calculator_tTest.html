Prove Differences. Compare Means. Is Group A really better than Group B?

🔍 What Is It?
A statistical hypothesis test to determine if the means of two unrelated groups are significantly different.
⏱️ When To Use?
When comparing two separate sets of data (e.g. Current Process vs New Process).
💡 Why Use It?
To prove with 95% confidence that an "Improvement" is real and not just random luck.
📋 Requirements
Normal Data. Independent Groups. Continuous Data (Measurements).
📐 Standard Equation
t = (x̄1 - x̄2) / √(s1²/n1 + s2²/n2)
🧠 Interpretation
P-Value < 0.05: Difference IS Significant (Reject Null).
P-Value > 0.05: No Difference.
🎯 Reference Values
Confidence Level usually 95% (Alpha = 0.05).
🚀 Phase Relevance
Analyze: Verify root cause.
Improve: Validate solution success.
