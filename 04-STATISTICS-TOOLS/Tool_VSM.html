VSM ARCHITECTURE STRATEGY

×

The Value Stream Map is the Master blueprint of Lean. It tracks three parallel flows to expose hidden inefficiencies.

📦 Material Flow

The movement of the physical item or patient. Our goal is Single Piece Flow—moving items one by one without stopping in queues.

📡 Information Flow

The scheduling signals (MRP, Electronic orders) that tell the process what to do. Centralized control minimizes 'Pushing' waste.

⌛ The Timeline

The 'Sawtooth' line at the bottom. It compares Processing Time (VA) vs. Waiting Time (NVA). VSM prioritizes reducing the Waiting Time.

🚨 Abnormality Mgmt

Using Flags and visual signals to identify when reliability drops below 90% or inventory swells beyond capacity.

VALUE CLASSIFICATION

VA - Value Added

Tasks that MUST be done to fulfill the patient's need. The patient is willing to "pay" for this time.

Example: Physician Consultation, Lab Result Analysis, Surgery.

NNVA - Necessary Non-Value Added

Tasks that don't add value to the patient but are required by regulation, safety standards, or current technology.

Example: Regulatory Compliance checks, Safety timeouts, Insurance verification.

NVA - Non-Value Added (WASTE)

Pure waste. If removed tomorrow, the patient would be happier and the outcome would be the same.

Example: Waiting for transport, searching for files, duplicate data entry.
