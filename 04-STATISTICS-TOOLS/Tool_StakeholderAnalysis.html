Influence Psychology: Logic vs Emotion

Master Black Belt Insight: Data satisfies the mind, but stories move the feet. Use the Logic to justify the decision, but use Emotion to ignite the action. Every "Blocker" is simply a stakeholder whose emotional connection to the current state is stronger than their trust in your future state.
