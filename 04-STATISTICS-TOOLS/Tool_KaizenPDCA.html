Kaizen & PDCA Philosophy

What is it?

Kaizen means "change for better." PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) is the scientific method behind that change.

Why use it?

To avoid "flavor of the month" fixes. PDCA ensures that logic and data drive improvements, not opinions.

When to add?

The "heartbeat" of the Improve phase. Every project solution should go through at least one PDCA pilot.

History of Origin

Walter Shewhart developed the cycle at Bell Labs; W. Edwards Deming took it to Japan (often called the Deming Cycle).

🍎 The Apple Stacking Pilot

Plan: Build a pyramid 5-layers high to save space. Do: Try on one shelf. Check: Count bruises after 24 hrs. Act: If bruising is high, lower to 3-layers and try again.

🏥 Hand Hygiene Compliance

Plan: Install gel dispensers at bedside instead of door. Do: Protocol pilot in Ward A. Check: Audit compliance via UV markers. Act: If compliance > 95%, rollout to Wards B & C.
