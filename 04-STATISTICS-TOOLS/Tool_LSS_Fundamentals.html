The LSS Evolution Timeline

Journey
1809
Carl Friedrich Gauss
The Bell Curve
The birth of the 'Normal Distribution.' The mathematical foundation of Six Sigma.
📐 Mathematics
1913
Henry Ford
The First Flow
Ford introduces the assembly line. He reduced chassis assembly from 12 hours to 93 minutes.
⚡ Speed (Lean)
1950s
Taiichi Ohno (Toyota)
TPS (Toyota Production System)
The birth of 'Muda' (Waste). Ohno defined the 7 Wastes. If the customer doesn't pay for it, cut it.
🎯 Efficiency (Lean)
1986
Bill Smith (Motorola)
Six Sigma is Born
Smith coined the term. The goal: 3.4 defects per million opportunities. Perfection becomes a metric.
📊 Quality (Six Sigma)
1995
Jack Welch (GE)
The Global Standard
Welch made Six Sigma famous. He proved it wasn't just for factories—it was for strategy and finance.
💼 Business Strategy
2002
Virginia Mason Medical Center
Healthcare Adopts Lean
The first hospital to fully embed TPS. They proved that 'Factory concepts' save human lives.
🏥 Patient Safety
Today
The Joint Commission
RPI (Robust Process Improvement)
Lean Six Sigma is now the gold standard for hospital accreditation and patient safety worldwide.
🛡️ Global Standard
