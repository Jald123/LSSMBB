The Evolution Journey

🏭 Factory Floor to 🏥 Bedside

🚗
1913 — The Foundation
Ford's Assembly Line
👤 Henry Ford & Frederick Taylor
Achievement: Reduced Model T production from 12 hours to 93 minutes. Introduced scientific management and standardized work processes. This was the birth of Flow thinking.
🏭
1948–1975 — The Toyota Revolution
Toyota Production System (TPS)
👤 Taiichi Ohno & Shigeo Shingo
Achievement: Born from necessity in post-war Japan with limited resources. Created Just-In-Time (JIT), Jidoka (automation with human touch), and defined the original 7 Wastes (Muda). This became the foundation of Lean.
📚
1988 — The Name is Born
"Lean" Terminology Coined
👤 John Krafcik (MIT Researcher)
Achievement: The term "Lean" first appeared in MIT's study comparing auto manufacturers. Later popularized by "The Machine That Changed the World" (1990) by Womack, Jones & Roos.
📱
1986 — Six Sigma Emerges
Motorola's Quality Revolution
👤 Bill Smith & Mikel Harry
Achievement: Created to fix quality issues in pagers and cell phones. Defined the 3.4 DPMO (Defects Per Million Opportunities) standard. Introduced the DMAIC methodology and Belt certification system.
💼
1995 — Corporate Powerhouse
General Electric Adoption
👤 Jack Welch (CEO)
Achievement: Made Six Sigma a strategic priority company-wide. Saved over $12 billion in five years. Proved that quality = profit. Made Six Sigma the global gold standard for business excellence.
⚡
2000s — The Fusion
Lean Six Sigma is Born
👤 Michael George (Author: "Lean Six Sigma")
Achievement: Combined Lean's speed with Six Sigma's precision. Created integrated toolkits, combined belt programs, and unified improvement frameworks. Best of both worlds: eliminate waste AND reduce variation.
🏥
2005–Present — Healthcare Revolution Healthcare
Patient Safety & Value-Based Care
👤 Virginia Mason, Cleveland Clinic, Mayo Clinic, Johns Hopkins
Achievement: Virginia Mason became the first to fully adopt Toyota's model for healthcare. Reduced patient wait times by 70%, eliminated medication errors, improved operating room turnaround. Now drives JCI accreditation standards and Value-Based Care initiatives globally. Healthcare LSS focuses on: Door-to-Balloon time, Surgical Safety Checklists, Discharge Planning, and Medication Administration.
