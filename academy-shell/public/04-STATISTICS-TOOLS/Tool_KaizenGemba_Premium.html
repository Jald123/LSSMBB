D.O.W.N.T.I.M.E. Guide

D - Defects: Time spent fixing errors. Example: Missing data on intake form.

O - Overproduction: Making more than needed. Example: Printing reports no one reads.

W - Waiting: People or parts idle. Example: Doctor waiting for lab results.

N - Non-Utilized Talent: Under-using skills. Example: Nurses doing heavy data entry.

T - Transportation: Moving items unnecessarily. Example: Patient transferred twice between wards.

I - Inventory: Excess stock. Example: 6 months of PPE in a small storage room.

M - Motion: Excessive person movement. Example: Walking to the other end of hall for a printer.

E - Extra Processing: Doing more than required. Example: Multiple signatures required for $50 spend.
