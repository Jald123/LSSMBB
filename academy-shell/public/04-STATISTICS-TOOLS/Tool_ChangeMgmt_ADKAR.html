Case Study: The "Paperless" Revolt

Context: A large clinic implemented a new scheduling system. Management sent everyone to a 4-hour training class immediately.

Result: Adoption was 12%. Staff printed schedules and typed them in later. Why?

Failure Point: They jumped to Knowledge (Training) while the staff had zero Desire (They liked paper). The ADKAR "Barrier Point" was at D.
Correction: Awareness

Shared data showing 20% of patients were double-booked due to paper errors (Patient Safety Issue).

Correction: Desire

Showed how the new system allows them to leave work 30 mins earlier (WIIFM - What's In It For Me).

Success

Once Desire was unlocked, the training (Knowledge) was actually absorbed. Adoption hit 95%.

A Awareness ⓘ
Awareness of Need for Change
Do they know why?
If Low: They think "This is just management noise."
Fix: Share the data. Show the burning platform.
D Desire ⓘ
Desire to Support Change
Do they want to?
If Low: "What's in it for me?" (WIIFM).
Fix: Address personal motivators vs inhibitors.
K Knowledge ⓘ
Knowledge of How to Change
Do they know how?
If Low: They feel stupid or unprepared.
Fix: Training sessions, Job Aids, FAQs.
A Ability ⓘ
Ability to Demonstrate Skills
Can they do it?
If Low: They understand the theory but fail in practice.
Fix: Hands-on coaching, Sandboxes, Time to practice.
R Reinforcement ⓘ
Reinforcement to Sustain
Will it stick?
If Low: They slide back to old habits next week.
Fix: Audits, Celebrations, Consequences.
